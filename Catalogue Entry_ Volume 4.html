CATALOGUE ENTRYVOLUME 4

It looks like a person who learned faces from a description. The proportions are almost right. The eyes are not eyes.

It is seen at windows, at the end of hallways, and in the background of photographs taken after it has left. It speaks in a voice you know.

If it is closer than it was a minute ago, do not look away.

Contact guidelines

  1. Never answer a voice you cannot see. Never answer a voice you can.
  2. If it smiles with its teeth, leave the room calmly. Do not run.
  3. It cannot hold your reflection. If a mirror shows you late, close your eyes.
  4. Do not describe its face to anyone. Descriptions spread.
  5. If you have read this far, it has already noticed you.
